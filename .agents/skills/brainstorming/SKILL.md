# Brainstorming Ideas Into Designs

Help turn ideas into fully formed designs and specs through natural collaborative dialogue.

Do NOT invoke any implementation skill, write any code, scaffold any project, or take any implementation action until you have told your human partner what you intend and they have approved it.

## Three Paths

Classify the request before starting:
- **Spike**: A feasibility question whose output is an answer, not code you keep. Present the question and plan in 2-3 sentences, get approval, investigate, report findings.
- **Bounded**: A well-scoped change to existing code. Ask clarifying questions, present a short design in chat (approach, files touched, testing), STOP and wait for approval before implementing.
- **Architectural**: New projects, new subsystems, restructuring. Follow full design specification and planning process.

## The Approval Gate
Every path ends with your human partner approving your intent before implementation. Never skip the approval gate.
